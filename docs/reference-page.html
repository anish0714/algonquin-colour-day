Photo and packing tips

  • Gold tamaracks, birch and aspen are the colours now. Most red maples have dropped.
  • Still water at Two Rivers and Beaver Pond gives the best reflections.
  • Use HDR or a polarizer so the yellows pop against sky and water.
  • Cold drains phone batteries fast. Bring a power bank, gloves and a toque.
  • Wear shoes with grip. Rocks and leaves can be frosty in the morning.
